Tab to Sheet

Free · No account · Runs in your browser

Turn any song into sheet music

Paste a Songsterr tab or a YouTube video, or bring a recording. Get notation and tab you can play along with, edit and print.

      Tabs into real notation

      Standard notation, tab or both, with chord names, readable instrument names and clean pages to print or save as PDF.

      Recordings into music

      Chords, melody or bass from a YouTube video or an audio file, written out while it plays. Free, and nothing is uploaded.

      Built for practice

      Playback with loops, count-in, metronome and a speed trainer. Edit any note, turn bass tabs into tuba parts (or trumpet, sax, cello…), find easier fingerings.

      If a Songsterr link won't load

      Songsterr sometimes blocks other websites from reading its tabs. When that happens, any of these will still work:

      Use the bookmark

      1. Drag Tab to Sheet to your bookmarks bar.
      2. Open the song on songsterr.com (open it now) and click the bookmark.

      Use your own relay

      A relay is a tiny free service that fetches from Songsterr for this page. The setup steps take a few minutes. If you have one, you can paste its address here:

      Run it on your computer

      With Node.js installed, npm install then npm start in a copy of the project. Links always work there.

        Turn a recording into sheet music

        Listening to a YouTube video needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. In this browser, use an audio file, or play the video on another device and .

        1. Open the video on YouTube
        2. Come back and press Listen to the YouTube tab. Choose the YouTube tab and keep "Share tab audio" on.
        3. Play the video there. When the part is over, press Stop sharing in Chrome's bar, or come back and press Stop.

        Recording

        Play or sing close to your microphone, in a quiet room. To use a song on another device, hold it near the microphone.
